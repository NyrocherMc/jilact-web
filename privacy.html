Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Jilact is an independent creator tool for organizing and publishing social-media content. This policy explains how Jilact handles information when you use the service.

Information we handle

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication session.
  • Content and scheduling details you provide, such as captions, media, planned dates and publishing preferences.
  • When you connect a social platform, the account details and authorization data needed to maintain that connection and carry out actions you request.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Jilact does not need or receive your social-platform password. Sign-in and authorization are handled through the relevant provider.

How we use information

We use information to provide Jilact, connect accounts you choose, prepare or publish content at your direction, show publishing status, provide support, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

When TikTok features are enabled and you connect TikTok, Jilact uses only the permissions shown on TikTok’s authorization screen for the requested integration, such as identifying the connected account and publishing content you direct Jilact to publish.

Service providers and sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the service: with infrastructure providers such as Supabase and Vercel, and with a social platform you connect when you request an action involving that platform. Those platforms process information under their own policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Storage, security and retention

Jilact may use browser storage for session and preference data. Information needed for account and publishing features may be processed by service providers in countries other than yours. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain information while it is needed to provide the service. To request deletion of your Jilact account data or connected-account data, email us from your account email address. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days, except for information we must retain by law or for security.

Your choices

You can stop using Jilact, revoke access through the connected platform, or request access, correction or deletion by contacting us. Revoking access may prevent related Jilact features from working.

Children

Jilact is not intended for children under 13, or the higher minimum age required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the effective date when we do. Questions or privacy requests: contact.jil.agent@gmail.com.
